# Forensic Analysis: Slot Provenance Zero-Gain Investigation

## 1. Executive Summary

Component C (**Slot Provenance Manager**, `SlotProvenanceManager`) was introduced in REACTOR v4 to maintain monotonic slot tracking, track revision provenance, and automatically restore missing required schema parameters from prior conversation turns after user interruptions or corrections.

In the official v4 ablation across all 278 tasks:
* **Full v4 Stack**: 58 / 278 (20.86%) Pass@1.
* **Without Slot Provenance (`wo_slot_provenance`)**: 58 / 278 (20.86%) Pass@1.
* **Measured Lift**: Exactly **0 tasks (0.00 pp lift)**.

An audit of all 267 tasks evaluated for conversational state and provenance (recorded in [`provenance_zero_gain.json`](file:///Users/atharvamendhulkar/desktop/reactor/artifacts/research_context/provenance_zero_gain.json)) reveals why this mechanism yielded zero measurable impact under frozen trajectory replay.

## 2. Empirical Findings

### A. Low Exercise Frequency in Recorded Trajectories
Across all 278 tasks, `slots_recovered` was incremented exactly **1 time**:
* Gemini 2.0 Flash rarely omitted required arguments that had been explicitly bound in earlier turns. Instead of omitting arguments, Gemini hallucinated placeholders (e.g. `""`, `"N/A"`, or invalid IDs).
* Because the keys were present in the model's tool call dictionary (`tc.arguments`), the schema inspection check `if req not in call_args:` evaluated to false in 277 / 278 tasks.

### B. Upstream Dominance in the Single Recovered Task
In the single task where a missing slot was successfully recovered from provenance history:
* The slot was restored into `call_args` before dispatch.
* However, the task still failed because:
  1. A subsequent tool call in the fixed trajectory executed with an invalid date parameter.
  2. The natural language assertion evaluator failed due to a missing closing confirmation.
* Partial argument restoration cannot convert a multi-step failure into a Pass@1 if subsequent pre-recorded turns fail.

### C. Offline Correction Invisibility
In τ-Voice trajectories, when a simulated user interrupted or corrected themselves (e.g. *"Wait, not Chicago, I meant Dallas"*), the gold benchmark evaluator evaluated whether the final database reflected Dallas.
In frozen replay:
* The model's recorded actions already either reflected the correction or failed to do so.
* Slot tracking records the provenance of Dallas with a higher revision number, but without the ability to dynamically re-prompt the LLM to generate a new proposal based on Dallas, the recorded proposal in the trajectory remained what Gemini originally emitted.

---

## 3. Methodological Conclusion

1. **State Tracking vs Action Generation**: Provenance tracking is a prerequisite for correct state maintenance in real-time conversational agents, but in an **offline replay setting**, state tracking alone cannot fix an action generated by an upstream LLM that ignored its own conversational context.
2. **Replay Benchmark Limitation**: The utility of slot provenance can only be evaluated in a **closed-loop generative harness**, where the agent's prompt context is dynamically constructed from active verified slots at each generation tick.
